Trexquant Superday / Topic B

An alpha factory built to reject its own output

An LLM agent loop that invents economic theses, implements them as formulas, and then spends most of its effort trying to prove them wrong.

Task specification01 / 17

One finished unit of work is an Alpha Card

An alpha signal is one number per stock per day. Only the ranking matters, so RankIC is the grade.

IN feature panel, point-in-time | universe | deterministic backtester | LLM token budget B | research corpus | production factor book OUT one Alpha Card

The eight parts of a card

  • Economic thesis - mechanism, counterparty, why not arbitraged
  • Pre-registered sign and horizon, hashed and timestamped
  • Formula over causal operators, with its canonical AST
  • Backtest report - RankIC, ICIR, Sharpe, turnover, MDD, decay
  • Honesty audit - marginal IC, Deflated Sharpe, t, PBO, peek spent
  • Red-Team report - which attacks ran, and each result
  • Verdict and lineage - thesis, parent formula, edit, generation
  • Provenance - fields used, read off the AST leaves

Measurement contract

Labelnext-day return, cross-sectionally demeaned
Timingfeatures to close of t, trade t+1 open, earn t+1 open to t+2 open
PrimaryRankIC, with IC and ICIR alongside
Decayh in {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 21}
Costs15 bps per side, swept 5 / 15 / 30 - robustness, not the grade

A rejected card is not discarded. It carries its death certificate into memory, and that is the substrate for improvement across generations.

Prototype scope is a faithful slice, not the whole design. The governing principle throughout: a partial result you understand beats a complete one you cannot defend.

The only stage that spends token budget deciding what not to try.

What each one decides

Stage S3 + Gate A05 / 17

Hypothesis and Gate A: commit to a story, and to a direction

The cheapest possible rejection, and the only place a prediction can still be made honestly.

IN evidence brief | idea family | prior lessons for this family OUT thesis + pre-registered sign, sha256-hashed into the ledger, then pass or reject

Gate A rubric: all five, or reject

  • A named mechanism
  • Who the counterparty is, and why they persistently lose
  • Why it has not already been arbitraged away
  • Horizon and regime
  • A falsifiable prediction, including the sign

Three teeth

  • The rubric is hard, not advisory
  • A different LLM client scores it. The author never grades itself
  • The sign is hashed now and checked against the realised sign later

Innovation: the pre-registered sign

Before any data is touched, the thesis is serialised to canonical JSON, sha256-hashed and timestamped into the trial ledger.

A signal that works only with the opposite sign to its own story is a thesis failure, not a discovery, and the mismatch never flips the sign to rescue it.

AlphaAgent aligns hypothesis and factor post hoc. AgonAlpha's reviewer audits sign logic after evaluation. We found nothing that commits a direction before evaluation and rejects on mismatch. This is our lead claim.

hash < bt
Prereg hash is written at a strictly earlier event index than the first backtest. Asserted.
0 calls
A blank rubric field is rejected structurally, with no LLM call spent at all

Author is not the judge, asserted by object identity. The test checks economics.client is not hypothesis.client, so a refactor cannot quietly collapse them into one conversation.

Stage S506 / 17

Implementation: bounded formula search

The thesis says "volume shock". It does not say whether the window is 5, 10 or 20 days. Only data can answer that, and that is exactly why it is dangerous.

IN thesis + sign | 35 causal operators | 35-formula zoo for duplicate detection | feature panel OUT one promoted formula, canonical AST, complexity, Tier-1 metrics, and up to 20 ledger rows

The inner loop

Coder > AST and complexity > free pre-filter (compile check, AST-duplicate vs zoo and memory) > Tier-1 RankIC on VAL-A > Judge > refine or promote.

Three bindings

  • Hard cap of 20 variants per thesis, enforced by the Planner
  • Every variant enters the trial ledger, and near-identical ASTs are clustered so 20 tweaks are not 20 independent bets
  • Fresh-fold confirmation. Search runs on Train + VAL-A. The winner is confirmed on VAL-B, which no variant ever touched, so this costs no holdout peek

This turns "we do formula search" from a liability into a rigor point: bounded, disclosed, deflated, and confirmed out of sample.

20
Exact measured maximum with a Judge injected to say "refine" forever
0
Trials charged by the pre-filter. Junk dies for free, before the Judge is paid

The operator library is the safety property

35 operators13 strictly trailing time-series, 5 cross-sectional, 17 element-wise
13 of 13time-series operators covered by a causality test: perturb a future row, assert every earlier output is bit-identical
Parser rejects__import__, attribute access, subscripts, comprehensions, lambdas, f-strings
Zoo25 Alpha101 + 10 classical, canonicalised and fingerprinted for duplicate detection

No operator can reach forward. Formula-level look-ahead is not policed here, it is structurally impossible. That is also why arbitrary Python factor evolution is excluded, not merely deferred.

Stage S607 / 17

Backtester: one engine, eight call sites, no decisions

The measuring instrument. It computes and returns a fixed dict. It never promotes, rejects or interprets.

IN signal (long or wide) | split | horizon | extra lag | cost bps | neutralize | subsample OUT Metrics: rank_ic, ic, icir, t_stat, sharpe, ann_return, turnover, mdd, n_days, n_obs, decay, sign

What it guarantees

  • Eight call sites, one engine: Tier-1, Tier-2, holdout, stress, cost sweep, curriculum, portfolio, ablation
  • Purge and embargo around every train and test boundary
  • HOLDOUT raises PermissionError unless a ledger peek token is presented
  • A HOLDOUT-tail purge stops a VAL-B label reaching across the seal
  • Regime labels use expanding-window thresholds only. A 2016 day cannot know 2024's volatility
  • No RNG. Two identical calls return bit-identical dicts, nested decay curve included

A look-ahead we found in our own engine. Regime labels originally used full-sample quantiles. Fixed at source, not patched in the test. A companion test confirms the broken version would have been detected, so the check is not vacuous.

Stage S7 / Gate B08 / 17

Gate B: is it new, and is it real?

Deterministic thresholds only. The order of the four steps is load-bearing, not cosmetic.

IN promoted formula | production factor book | the whole trial ledger | pre-registered sign OUT verdict + honesty audit: marginal IC, DSR, t, PBO, effective N, peek id, what the peek scored

Four ordered steps

  • Orthogonalise against the book, per day, cross-sectionally. Keep the residual
  • Novelty: marginal IC of the residual. Free, so a clone dies here having spent nothing
  • Statistics: Deflated Sharpe on the residual, t > 3, PBO, deflated by the run-wide effective trial count
  • Rationed holdout peek, also on the residual, so what was spent is auditable

The core argument11 / 17

Five failure modes, five mechanisms, and what each does not catch

The design's real content is knowing which mechanism catches which failure, and which one it sails straight past.

Failure modeCaught byDoes NOT catch it
Look-ahead and leakage Causal operator library (structural), timing contract, purge and embargo, extra-lag test, delivery shift Deflated Sharpe and PBO. A deliberately leaky oracle at Sharpe 35 survives both. Gençay, arXiv 2608.27734
Selection and multiple testing Deflated Sharpe vs the effective trial count, PBO, variant cap, fresh fold, rationed holdout Any amount of economic reasoning. A beautiful story does not reduce your trial count
Story-fitting
right answer, wrong reason		 Pre-registered sign, counterparty rubric, author is not the judge IC, Sharpe and DSR all pass happily. A signal with the wrong sign for its own story is statistically excellent
Redundancy and crowding AST duplicate check in S5, then marginal IC against the production book in Gate B All of the above. A perfect clone of an incumbent factor passes every statistical and economic test there is
Regime and cost fragility Red-Team fixed menu: regime split, cost sweep, size tercile, liquidity filter, universe edge Everything measured in-sample. A full-period RankIC averages a bull and a bear into a number describing neither
Broken data source External reconciliation only. We found one. Slide 13. Every mechanism in this table. It contaminates the universe, not any one factor. Nothing throws an exception

Formula-level look-ahead is structurally impossible. Every operator is trailing-window or same-day cross-sectional. No operator can reach forward, and all 13 time-series operators carry a causality test that proves it.

Statistical gates catch over-searching. They do not catch cheating. Different failures, different machinery. Deflating a leaky signal by its trial count gives you a rigorous number about a fiction.

The core argument12 / 17

Three budgets, and the conflict between the first and the third

The prompt names the token budget. It is real, but it is not the scarce resource that decides whether a discovery is true.

BudgetSpent byIs a backtest expensive here?How we economise
1. LLM tokens Hypothesis, Coder, Judge, Red-Team, Reflection No. A backtest is a Python call returning a small dict. Near zero tokens Bandit funds productive families. The free pre-filter kills junk before we pay a Judge
2. Compute Running backtests Barely. The Tier-2 CSCV battery is modestly heavier Heavy CSCV only on finalists
3. Statistical integrity Every selecting backtest is one trial. Every holdout peek is irreplaceable Yes, invisibly. Nothing gets slower, nothing errors. The bar for everything else just rises Variant cap, append-only ledger, Deflated Sharpe, 12 peeks ever

The conflict. We have not found this stated anywhere.

Budgets 1 and 3 pull in opposite directions. Token efficiency rewards finding the best candidate in fewer evaluations. Statistical integrity punishes you for every evaluation made along the way.

Any technique that makes search more sample-efficient at maximising a noisy objective is, by the same token, more efficient at overfitting.

This is why MCTS is on our roadmap rather than in our loop.

Bad examples13 / 17

Three ways to be wrong: data, statistics, economics

Told in three beats each. The first is not a demonstration. It happened to us, on the data we were handed.

1. DATA INTEGRITY. REAL.
The universe source was structurally broken
NaiveThe supplied constituent file passes every superficial check: 37 snapshots, exactly 200 names in each, dead companies retained, inclusion and exclusion events tracked.
Caught byExternal reconciliation against NSE, not by any gate.
42 stocks that were already trading in 2015 and sit in today's most-liquid 200 never appear in any snapshot: RELIANCE, TCS, SBIN, ONGC, MARUTI, TATASTEEL, COALINDIA, POWERGRID, SUNPHARMA, TITAN.
100 of 315 names carry zero events. The signature of a change-log replayed onto an incomplete seed, then padded back to 200 with mid-caps.
Why it landsThe missing names are systematically the largest and most liquid in India. Every liquidity, size and capacity feature would have been computed on a sample with its top end sawn off. DSR, PBO, purge and the lag test all pass it silently.
FixAbandon constituent data entirely. Rebuild from daily bhavcopy as the top 200 by trailing turnover: point-in-time and survivorship-free by construction. Coverage slope 2.6e-17 per day, and it recovers all six heavyweights on 100 percent of days.
2. STATISTICAL
Look-ahead leakage
NaiveA factor that lets same-day or forward information into the return window posts a spectacular Tier-1 RankIC. A signal that is quietly its own label scores 1.0000.
Caught byStructure, three times over. The causal operator library makes it inexpressible. The panel self-test detects it and shows it collapsing to -0.060 one day later. Red-team test 5 destroys it: 1.0000 to 0.0018 on one extra day of lag.
Teaching pointThe Deflated Sharpe would have passed it. Gençay's leaky oracle posts Sharpe 35 and survives DSR and PBO completely. Statistical gates catch over-searching. They do not catch cheating.
FixClose the surface rather than measure it. Causal operators only, an explicit timing contract, purge and embargo, and a lag test that can kill on its own. It is also why arbitrary-Python factor evolution stays out of scope.
3. ECONOMIC
Right answer, wrong reason
NaiveA data-mined signal posts a respectable IC and passes every statistical gate, but its realised sign is the opposite of what its own thesis predicted. The story said crowded stocks underperform. The money was on the other side.
Caught byThe pre-registered sign. The thesis is hashed and timestamped before any data is touched, and a test asserts the hash lands at a strictly earlier event index than the first backtest.
VerdictRejected as a thesis failure, not a discovery, and the sign is never flipped to rescue it. That is the tempting move, and refusing it is the whole point. Costs zero holdout peeks.
Teaching pointNo purely statistical gate would ever flag this. IC, Sharpe, DSR, PBO and the red team all pass it. It is the one failure mode where the economics is the test.

Evaluation14 / 17

Grading the factory, not the signal

Grading one signal is the Alpha Card. Grading the factory asks entirely different questions.

Five dimensions

Yieldhypotheses per accepted card, tokens per accepted alpha, marginal IC added per generation, diversity
HonestyFDR = accepted but fails holdout, over accepted. DSR distribution. Sign agreement rate
Efficiencyreal alpha per token. The headline objective
Gate valuethe ablation: catch rate, false-kill rate, FDR with the gate on and off
Real learningerror volume trend, not error type

Detecting fake learning

Factor-mining agents can look like they are learning while only their mistakes mature: conceptual, then operational, then strategic, with total error volume barely moving.

So we track rejections per generation and per-gate pass rate, not the changing character of failures. Falling volume is improvement. Same volume in new clothes is drift. We will report which one we see, including if it is the unflattering answer.

The ablation: designed and specified not yet run

Seed a pool of about 40 factors with documented ground truth: 10 genuinely predictive, 10 pure noise, 10 deliberately overfit, 10 deliberately leaky. Run the pool with each gate enabled, then disabled.

Report per gate: catch rate, false-kill rate, and headline FDR on versus off.

This makes complexity self-justifying rather than asserted. We did not add gates because papers have them. We measured what each one catches. The corollary is the real argument for pruning: we can only make that argument for gates we actually ablated.

What we cannot yet show, and will not pretend to

  • No accepted Alpha Card exists. The full accept path is exercised end to end on a fixture, but not on a real thesis
  • No ablation numbers. Designed above, none measured. There are no placeholder figures anywhere in this deck
  • No fake-learning plot. It needs multi-generation run data that does not exist yet
  • Small samples will make these numbers illustrative, not conclusive, and we will label them that way
Positioning15 / 17

Honest novelty: two claims conceded, two that survive

Two of our four original headline claims were already published. Conceding them with citations is a stronger position than a bluff.

ClaimStatusPosition
Pre-registered sign and counterparty gate genuinely novel Our lead claim. AlphaAgent does post-hoc semantic alignment. AgonAlpha's reviewer audits sign logic after evaluation. Nothing found commits a direction before evaluation and rejects on mismatch. A post-hoc check can be talked around. A hashed commitment cannot.
Three budgets, and their conflict novel Token efficiency and statistical integrity pull in opposite directions. Not found stated anywhere, and it produces a falsifiable consequence: MCTS goes on the roadmap, not in the loop.
Fixed-menu, rejection-only red team differentiated Adversarial review already exists in AgonAlpha and FactorMAD. Ours is a fixed, parameterised menu, so no arbitrary code runs and every attack reproduces, and it is provably rejection-only, so it cannot inflate the trial count.
Statistical-rigor gates wired into fitness anticipated Not novel. Conceded. Gençay, arXiv 2608.27734, got there first. We cite it and lead instead with what DSR does not catch: their leaky oracle posts Sharpe 35 and survives DSR and PBO completely. Statistical gates are table stakes now. Knowing their blind spot is the contribution.

Limitations, stated before you find them

  • No accepted card and no ablation numbers yet. The two deliverables that need a full loop run
  • One market, one universe, prototype scale. No evidence about production breadth
  • No point-in-time fundamentals, so whole feature families are unavailable. A rigor choice that costs coverage
  • CSCV applies a light purge, not a full embargo. Strategies are not refit inside splits, so leakage is limited to label overlap at block seams
  • The LLM tier is a free-tier model chain, not a frontier model. The architecture is model-agnostic. Thesis quality is not
